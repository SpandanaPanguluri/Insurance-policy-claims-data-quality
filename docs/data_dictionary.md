# Data Dictionary

## policy.csv
Policy term, account, LOB, risk state, effective/expiration dates, status, written premium.

## coverage.csv
Coverage-level rows linked to policy.

## policy_transaction.csv
Policy transactions such as new business, endorsement, cancellation, with premium impact.

## claim.csv
Claim-level loss date, status, incurred, paid, and reserve amounts.

## reporting_policy_summary.csv
Policy-level downstream reporting layer used for reconciliation.
